Hello, I'm

Nishant Luitel

PhD student · Rochester Institute of Technology

I am a PhD student at Rochester Institute of Technology, advised by Dr. Rui Li. I study how learning systems can model uncertainty, organize specialized expertise, and generate useful outputs.

I received my bachelor’s degree in Computer Engineering with Distinction from Pulchowk Campus, Tribhuvan University. Before joining RIT, I worked as a Research Assistant with the TOGAI group at NAAMII, studying deep learning for blind-sweep obstetric ultrasound and fetal assessment.
