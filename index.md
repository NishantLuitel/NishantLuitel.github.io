I am a PhD student at [Rochester Institute of Technology](https://www.rit.edu/), advised by [Dr. Rui Li](https://www.rit.edu/ai/directory/rxlics-rui-li).

I received my bachelor's degree in Computer Engineering with Distinction from [Pulchowk Campus, Tribhuvan University](https://pcampus.edu.np/). Before joining RIT, I worked as a Research Assistant with [TOGAI group at NAAMII](https://naamii.org/research/research-groups/togai_transforming_global_health_with_ai), studying deep learning for blind-sweep obstetric ultrasound and fetal assessment.
